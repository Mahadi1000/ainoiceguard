# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Ainoiceguard to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their
age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of
experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation. We pledge to act and interact in ways that support
an open, friendly, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- being kind and patient, especially with newcomers;
- respecting different opinions, experiences and setups;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Examples of behaviour that is not acceptable:

- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention;
- trolling, insults, personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing other people's private information (including their voice or
  audio recordings) without their explicit permission;
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any other channel run by the project — and when someone
represents the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer
privately through the e-mail address on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Mahadi1000). All reports
will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.
They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and will explain why
when appropriate. Depending on the severity, responses range from a private
warning, to a temporary ban, to a permanent ban from the project's spaces.
